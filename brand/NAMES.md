# Name candidates

Collision check method: quick web searches (Nov 1, 2026) for each name plus "app" / "company" / travel context. This is a first screen, **not** a trademark clearance. Before committing, search USPTO (TESS), EUIPO eSearch and UKIPO in Class 9 (software), 36 (financial/claims services) and 45 (legal-adjacent services), and check the .com / .app domains and @handles.

| # | Name | One-line rationale | Collision screen |
|---|------|--------------------|------------------|
| 1 | **DelayProof** | Says the job in two words: your delay, proven. "Proof" carries the public-record idea without saying "blockchain". | No app or company found under this name. Only generic "Delay" automation tools (Zapier, Aragon) surfaced. Descriptive, so a trademark would be weaker to enforce. |
| 2 | **Ondue** | "On due" / echoes "overdue": the flight was late, the money is due. Short, ownable, easy to say on video. | Nothing found for "Ondue". Nearby: Onde (a taxi-app platform) and the ONDU ETF ticker. Low risk, but needs a tagline to explain it. |
| 3 | **FlightJury** | Makes the validator network instantly graspable: a jury looks at the facts and rules. | No app found. "Jury" is crowded in legal tech (jury-selection apps). Risk: sounds like a lawsuit, which may scare casual travelers. |
| 4 | **Gatecase** | Your case, opened at the gate. | No app found, but "Gate" names are crowded, including **Gate.io, a crypto exchange**. That link is the wrong signal for a crypto-invisible brand. |
| 5 | **Fairwing** | Fairness + flight. Warm, consumer-friendly. | No exact match, but close to "Freewing" (flight-assistant app) and FairFly (airfare tracking). Easy to confuse. |
| 6 | **Plainwing** | Plain-language rulings for flyers. | No collisions found. Weak meaning; doesn't hint at money or proof. |
| 7 | **Wingcourt** | Court for your wing (flight). | No collisions found. "Court" overpromises legal force the ruling doesn't have. |
| 8 | **Overdue** | Perfect pun (late flight / money owed). | Common English word used by books, podcasts and fintech features; impossible to own in search. |
| 9 | **Owed** | The emotional payoff in one word. | Generic word; refund apps (Payback, AirFair, Refund.me) already fill the "you're owed" search space. Hard to trademark or rank for. |
| 10 | **Tarmac** | Where every delay story happens. | **Taken**: an existing travel-software company called Tarmac (CB Insights profile) and HEC Paris's "Tarmac" incubator. Reject. |

## Recommendation: top 3

1. **DelayProof (top pick).** It already is the working name, it explains itself in a TikTok caption with no help, and "Proof" is honest about what the product makes: a verified public record, not a legal judgment. No direct collision was found. The weakness is that it's descriptive. Fix that with the distinctive mark (the ticket-stub check) and register the logo plus wordmark together.
2. **Ondue.** The most ownable and brandable name, and the best fallback if DelayProof fails clearance or the domain is unavailable. It needs a line under it: "Ondue: find out if your delayed flight owes you."
3. **FlightJury.** The best metaphor for the validator network. Good as a feature name inside DelayProof: "Your case goes to the FlightJury: five independent validators, one ruling."

Suggested use: **DelayProof** as the brand, **"the network"** as the plain-English name for the validators, and FlightJury kept as a possible campaign line.
